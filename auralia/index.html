Put it on your phone

Four quiet steps.

Auralia includes a dramatic Lock image and a calmer matching Home image for every theme.

iPhone

  1. Save the wallpaper.
  2. Open Settings → Wallpaper.
  3. Choose the Auralia LOCK image.
  4. Use the matching HOME image for the Home Screen.

Dark or Tinted icons suit Auralia particularly well.

Android

  1. Save the wallpaper.
  2. Open Wallpaper & style or long-press the Home Screen.
  3. Apply LOCK to the Lock Screen.
  4. Apply HOME to the Home Screen.

Dark or monochrome themed icons work beautifully with the set.
