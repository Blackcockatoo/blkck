AURALIA — Android

1. Open the wallpaper you want.
2. Save it to your phone.
3. Long-press the Home Screen or open Settings → Wallpaper & style.
4. Set the Auralia LOCK image for Lock Screen.
5. Set the matching Auralia HOME image for Home Screen.

For the cleanest B$S look:
• use dark or monochrome themed icons where available
• keep widgets minimal
• disable scrolling/parallax wallpaper if it crops the composition badly
• center the main subject before applying

The files are universal 9:16 masters. Android manufacturers may crop them slightly differently.
