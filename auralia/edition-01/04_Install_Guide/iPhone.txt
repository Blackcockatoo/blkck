AURALIA — iPhone

1. Open the wallpaper you want.
2. Save it to Photos.
3. Open Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper.
4. Choose the Auralia LOCK image.
5. Set it as your Lock Screen.
6. Tap Customize Home Screen and choose the matching Auralia HOME image.

For the cleanest B$S look:
• use Dark or Tinted app icons
• keep widgets minimal
• avoid extra wallpaper blur unless you prefer it
• position the crop so the main figure stays clear

The files are universal 9:16 masters. iOS may crop a small amount depending on model.
