Teacher Pack

For teachers · ESOs · support staff
Problem

Teachers need fast, practical behaviour templates and rarely have time to build them — planning meetings, handovers and support notes eat the week.

Solution

Seven printable behaviour-support tools (the Teacher's Secret Cheatsheet), free, no login, Victoria-aware, ready to photocopy.

Use

Planning meetings, staff handovers, support notes, transition moments and quick classroom resets.

Safety

Practical templates, not legal or clinical advice. They support existing school processes; they don't replace formal plans made with qualified staff.

Meta-Pet

For schools · parents · Years 3–6
Problem

Kids are surrounded by dopamine apps built to extract attention, money and data — and schools have almost no child-safe digital alternatives to point to.

Solution

A local-first digital companion with no ads, no trackers and no accounts, where sessions are designed to end rather than to trap.

Use

Pattern noticing, responsibility and care routines, reflection and short teacher-led classroom sessions — a full pilot pack exists for schools.

Safety

A working prototype with honest status labels, not a finished commercial product. All play data stays on the device and can be deleted by clearing browser data.

Try Meta-Pet → School Pilot Pack →

Black Wing Crew

For music fans · venues · collaborators
Problem

Independent music releases disappear online within days — a stream, a post, then silence.

Solution

Songs become a world: lyric posters, QR street drops, visuals, chants and a mythology that keeps the album alive off-platform.

Use

The Neon Venom LP (BSS-001) streams on the official site while its posters and QR codes live on walls, in packs and in print.

Safety

All-ages visual world; street materials are designed for legal poster spots and community boards.
