Music that doesn't disappear

Black Wing Crew turns each release into a world: songs, lyric posters, QR street drops, visuals and mythology — so the music keeps living off-platform.

Current release

  • Neon Venom LP (BSS-001, 2026) — streaming on the official site with full lyric posters and street print assets.
  • Frankston → Fuji — a bilingual sister-city song project connecting Frankston and Japan.
  • @blkck2 on YouTube — music videos, art drops and studio process.

The method

  • Every song ships with printable poster art
  • QR codes on the street open the music directly
  • Lyrics, chants and characters form one connected world
