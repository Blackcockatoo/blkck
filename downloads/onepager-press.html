Blue Snake Studios

An independent creative studio in Frankston, Victoria — art, music, child-safe learning tools and symbolic systems, built as one connected world.

Boilerplate

Blue Snake Studios (B$S) is the working studio of The Moss Man, an independent artist based in Frankston, Victoria. The studio spans visual art (the Auralia series and ten online gallery rooms), music (Black Wing Crew and the 2026 Neon Venom LP), privacy-first education software (Meta-Pet), free teacher resources, and an original visual number system (Moss 60). Everything is published openly at blkck2.com — no ads, no trackers, no student accounts.

Key projects

  • Meta-Pet — privacy-first digital learning companion; school pilot pack ready (bluesnakestudios.com)
  • Black Wing Crew — Neon Venom LP, BSS-001, streaming with lyric posters and QR street drops
  • Moss 60 — an original base-60 visual number system with an interactive app
  • Teacher Pack — seven free printable behaviour-support templates
  • Frankston → Fuji — bilingual sister-city song project
