Creative learning tools for schools, libraries & councils

Blue Snake Studios offers privacy-first creative learning tools, printable classroom resources, and guided workshops for schools, libraries, councils and community programs.

What we offer

  • Meta-Pet — a privacy-first digital learning companion. Browser-based, teacher-led, no ads, no trackers, no student accounts. School pilot pack available.
  • Teacher Pack — seven free printable behaviour-support templates for teachers and education support staff.

Privacy position
