Meta-Pet · Blue Snake Studios · Privacy summary for schools & families

No ads. No trackers. No student accounts.

Meta-Pet is a browser-based, local-first digital learning companion. This page answers the privacy questions schools and parents actually ask — in plain language.

What data is stored?Only what the child creates in play: pet progress, customisation and settings. No names, no accounts, no profiles.
Where is it stored?Locally, in the browser on the device being used. Nothing is transmitted to the studio or any third party by default.
How is it deleted?Clear the browser's site data for the app. That removes everything. Anyone with device access — teacher, parent or child — can do it.
What if the cache is cleared accidentally?The pet and its progress are gone. There is no cloud copy — that is the trade-off of local-first design.
What is never collected?Names, emails, photos, voice, location, contacts, behavioural profiles, advertising identifiers. There is no mechanism to collect them.
Are there social features?No chat, no feeds, no followers, no comments. There is no in-app channel through which a stranger could contact a child.
Are there manipulative design patterns?No loot boxes, no random-reward compulsion loops, no infinite scroll. Sessions are designed to end.
Who supervises classroom use?The teacher, under the school's normal ICT and behaviour policies. School use is teacher-led and time-bounded.

For ICT review

Meta-Pet runs in a standard browser with no installation, no student login and no third-party ad or analytics SDKs. A full governance pack — ICT privacy review checklist, data inventory, parent/carer notice and pilot runbook — is available at bluesnakestudios.com/schools.
