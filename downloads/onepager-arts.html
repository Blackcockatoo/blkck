Blue Snake Studios · The Moss Man · Frankston, Victoria

An independent visual world, built in Frankston

Original paintings, digital art series, printable street works and ten themed online gallery rooms — one artist, one connected universe.

The work

  • Auralia series — eleven original artworks; the studio's central character. Available as high-resolution prints and A3 editions.
  • Ten gallery rooms — an online archive spanning paintings, mythology, music art, humour and street print work at blkck2.com.
  • Street print practice — QR poster trails and A4 print packs that move the work off-screen and into public space.
  • Moss 60 — a visual number system: base-60 geometry as drawable, exhibitable pattern work.
