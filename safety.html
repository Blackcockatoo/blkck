Plain language · For schools, parents and ICT officers

What B$S is — and is not.

Blue $nake Studio makes creative and educational material. Before any school, council or family uses it, here are the boundaries, stated as plainly as we can state them.

What B$S is not

  • Not clinical therapy. Nothing here diagnoses, treats or replaces mental-health or allied-health services.
  • Not legal advice. Teacher templates and documents are practical tools, not legal instruments.
  • Not medical advice. No content here should guide medical decisions.
  • Not a behaviour replacement plan. The Teacher Pack supports existing school processes; it does not replace formal plans made with qualified staff.
  • Not collecting student data. No student accounts, no profiles, no analytics on children. Meta-Pet stores play data locally on the device only.
  • Not a social platform. No chat, no feeds, no followers, no comments, no way for strangers to contact a child through anything we build.
  • Not a gambling or dopamine loop. No loot boxes, no random rewards engineered for compulsion, no infinite scroll, no dark patterns to extend screen time.

What B$S is

  • Creative support. Art, music, characters and worlds that give kids and adults something to make, not just consume.
  • Printable tools. Behaviour-support templates, colouring pages, glyph sheets and posters — free to download and print.
  • Reflective activities. Pattern noticing, care routines and short guided tasks designed to end, not to trap attention.
  • Privacy-first prototypes. Browser-first, local-first software with no ads and no trackers, published with honest status labels.
  • Workshop material. Bookable creative sessions for schools, libraries and community programs, run under the venue's normal supervision policies.
  • Pattern-based learning. Moss 60 geometry, symbolic systems and visual mathematics presented as things to explore and draw.

Who is responsible when this is used with children?

The supervising adult — teacher, parent or program leader — under their normal policies. Blue $nake Studio tools have no in-app communication channels, so there is no vector for strangers to reach children through them. During school pilots, sessions are teacher-led and time-bounded, and the school's ICT and behaviour policies apply as usual.

If a complex individual need comes up while using any of this material, refer it to qualified professionals. That is a boundary we state everywhere, on purpose.

The data position, in three sentences.

Nothing we ship requires an account, and nothing collects names, emails, photos, location or behavioural profiles of children. Meta-Pet play data lives in the browser on the device being used and can be deleted by clearing the site data. The full detail is in the Privacy Policy and the School Pilot Pack.
