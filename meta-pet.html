01 · What it is

A digital companion that gives attention back.

Meta-Pet is a concept in active development by The Moss Man / Blue $nake Studio. It combines a digital pet care model with STEAM learning mechanics, pattern recognition tasks and a symbolic identity system powered by Moss 60 base-60 geometry.

The core idea: a digital companion that children can care for, customise and learn with — without any of the design patterns that extract attention, money or data from children or families.

The prototype is available at bluesnakestudios.com. It runs in the browser — no download, no app store, no accounts. Open and use immediately. School pilot enquiries are welcome — contact the studio directly.

03 · Straight answers

The short version, for schools and IT officers.

The full detail lives in the privacy policy and the school pilot pack. These are the plain answers up front.

What data is stored?

Only what the child creates in play: pet progress, customisation and settings. No names, no accounts, no profiles.

Where is it stored?

Locally, in the browser on the device being used. Nothing is transmitted to the studio or to any third party by default.

Who can delete it?

Anyone with access to the device — the teacher, the parent or the child. Clearing the browser's site data removes everything.

What happens if the browser cache is cleared?

The pet and its progress are gone. There is no cloud copy, which is the trade-off of local-first design.

What is exported, and by whom?

Nothing is exported automatically. Any export is a deliberate, teacher- or parent-initiated action on the device.

What is never collected?

Names, emails, photos, voice, location, contacts, behavioural profiles, advertising identifiers. There is no mechanism to collect them.

Who is responsible during a classroom incident?

The supervising teacher, under the school's normal ICT and behaviour policies. Meta-Pet has no chat, social or contact features, so there is no in-app channel for incidents to arrive through.
