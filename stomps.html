01 / The original

Not born
of the label.

An original Frankstonite. What the kids now call an eshay, he was before the name had a following.

He doesn't wear a persona. The cap, jacket, shorts and runners are familiar, but the clothes aren't what define him. It's the way he sets his feet, reads the room and keeps his own counsel.

He is grounded, streetwise, alert, defiant and self-possessed. B$S isn't giving him a gangster mythology, invented exploits or a borrowed biography. The character portrait gives him the respect of being himself.

Don't call it a trend.
He was here first.

02 / The code

Play.
For. Keeps.

PFK. Play For Keeps. STOMPS is introduced here as an original member. His ties are part of his history, not a slogan for strangers to borrow.

Honour and respect the person. Do not dilute him into a friendly mascot, a recruitment pitch, or a man who owes the public his attention.

B$S sponsors the creative treatment. It does not claim to speak on his behalf. That distinction matters.
