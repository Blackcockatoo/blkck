Privacy Policy

blkck2.com · Blue $nake Studio · The Moss Man

Last updated: 23 August 2026

Short version: blkck2.com has no advertising, audience-analytics script, portal login or profile database. Some tools keep state locally in your browser. As with any hosted website, the hosting service processes ordinary page-request information; external embeds and assets may also receive a request when they load.

Who we are

Blue $nake Studio is an independent creative practice based in Frankston, Victoria, Australia, operated by The Moss Man. Our creative portal lives at blkck2.com.

Contact: bluesssnakestudio@gmail.com or blkck2@gmail.com

What information is involved

Blue $nake Studio does not operate a sign-up, account or profile database for this portal, and does not add advertising pixels or audience analytics. The hosting service necessarily processes ordinary request information when a page or file is requested, such as an IP address, time, requested path, user agent and related technical details. Its retention and access controls are governed by the hosting service.

If you choose an email link, your message and address are handled by your email provider and the receiving mailbox. Nothing is submitted merely by opening an email link.

Cookies

Blue $nake Studio's own scripts on this portal do not intentionally set cookies. External services loaded from some pages may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

Browser localStorage

Several interactive pages use browser storage to remember local state, such as checklist progress, intro preferences, entitlements or save state. That application state remains in the browser unless a person deliberately uses a separate sharing or contact pathway. It can be removed by clearing site data in the browser.

The portal also uses a service worker and normal browser caching so files can load faster or remain available offline. Clearing cookies and site data through the browser removes the local records covered by that browser's controls.

Third-party services

Some pages load external content, including a click-to-load YouTube embed, externally generated QR images and web fonts in a published document. When that content is requested, its provider may receive technical request information such as an IP address, user agent and referrer and may apply its own privacy terms.

Links to other B$S sites and unrelated platforms open those services under their own policies. No advertising network or audience-analytics script is intentionally operated by Blue $nake Studio on the blkck2.com portal.

Children

The public portal does not require a student account or student personal details by default. Child-facing and school tools should be used under adult supervision and the setting's normal privacy, safeguarding and ICT processes.

Meta-Pet's school experience has its own, more detailed privacy and governance information at metapet.school. Its classroom state can remain local to the browser and be cleared by the supervising adult; student personal data is not transmitted to Blue $nake Studio by default.

Your rights

There is no blkck2.com portal account or profile record to manage. You control local application state through your browser's site-data controls. For questions about information you deliberately emailed to the studio, contact bluesssnakestudio@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the portal's data model or external services change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change. The current design commitment is no advertising, no audience profiling and no unnecessary collection.
