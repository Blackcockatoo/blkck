The arcade.

Playable games from Blue $nake Studio, built in the same mythic universe as the art, music and learning tools.

Each cabinet below links to where the game actually lives. Nothing here is a copy — the games are hosted where they are built, so what you play is always the current build.

  • Live

    Bubble Hex

    Trap enemies in bubbles, chain-pop them for score multipliers, collect VENOM letters and survive twelve gothic neon chambers.

    Move with A / D, jump with Space, blow a bubble with X. Touch and gamepad supported.

    Play Bubble Hex →
  • Live

    Monkey Invaders

    Arcade shooter. Hold the line against descending invaders, then break the boss waves. Served through the Blue $nake Studios site.

    Hold ◀ / ▶ to move, hold 🍌 to fire. Landscape recommended on phones.

    Play Monkey Invaders →
  • Enhanced build

    Monkey Invaders — enhanced

    A separate, further-developed engine with bosses, extra enemy types, events, pickups, secrets and progression. Still changing week to week.

    This is not the same build as the one above. Expect differences.

    Try the enhanced build →
  • Live

    B$S Six-Face Word Cube

    Drag to spin a 3D artwork cube with six word-grid faces. Free in the browser, no login.

    Drag to rotate. Works with mouse and touch.

    Play Word Cube →
  • Prototype

    Oracle Warden

    The Moss 60 glyph engine as a playable symbolic puzzle. Rough edges — this one is still an experiment.

    Keyboard and pointer.

    Open Oracle Warden →

Two Monkey Invaders?

Yes — deliberately. The Blue $nake Studios site serves an older standalone port of the game. The enhanced build is a separate, more advanced engine developed in its own repository. They are different games at different stages, so both are listed rather than silently swapped.

Not part of MetaPet.school

The arcade is not available through metapet.school. That domain runs a deliberately constrained classroom build, and games are blocked there along with the rest of the consumer experience — not merely hidden from the menu.

If you are here for the classroom product, start at the MetaPet page or Schools / Council instead.
