What this page is

A marker in the archive.

Frankston 2035 is not a policy claim or official council plan. It is a Blue $nake Studio project page for organising a local future story: a place where creative education, safe technology, community art and cultural bridge work can be found under one name.

Possible outputs

Things it can hold.

  • Workshop proposals for schools, libraries and youth/community spaces.
  • Frankston-focused posters, printable artefacts, QR trails and public-facing explainers.
  • Connections to the Frankston → Fuji sister-city learning bridge.
  • Proof of privacy-first, child-safe creative tools that can be tested locally.
